Discover handcrafted designs inspired by Pilipino culture

Native bags in the Philippines are celebrated for their eco-friendly craftsmanship and sustainable materials. Handwoven by local artisans across the archipelago, these pieces range from classic straw market Bags to modern, high-end designer clutches using indigenous fibers, wood, and mother-of-pearl.

"Bags with a Story, Woven by Tradition"

Each bag you’ll find here is more than just an accessory—it is a living piece of heritage. These creations embody centuries of tradition and artistry.

About Us

Where They Come From

  • Our bags are made from natural fibers such as abaca and other locally sourced materials.
  • The designs are inspired by tribal traditions, often carrying symbolic patterns that reflect nature, dreams, and spirituality.
  • Every stitch and weave tells a story passed down through generations.

Why It Matters

  • Cultural Preservation: By supporting these native bags, you help keep alive the weaving traditions of tribes like the T’boli and Bagobo.
  • Support: Each purchase directly contributes to the sustainable income of indigenous artisans, empowering them to provide for their families while continuing their craft.
  • Eco-Friendly: Made from natural fibers, these bags are environmentally conscious alternatives to mass-produced products.

Our Mission

We believe that these cultural items should not fade away in the face of modernization. By bringing them to a wider audience, we aim to protect heritage, support communities, and share the beauty of Mindanaoan artistry with the world.
